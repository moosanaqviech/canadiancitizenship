## Full description (4,000 max)

Walk into your citizenship test calm and ready.

Canadian Citizenship Test 2026 helps you learn what's in Discover Canada, the official study guide, one short lesson at a time. Then you can check yourself with mock tests in the same format as the real test.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 12 chapters, from the Oath and your rights to Canada's history, government, elections, justice system, symbols, economy and regions
• Short lesson cards you can finish in a few minutes
• 120 original practice questions, each with a clear explanation
• Chapter quizzes with no timer. Retake any chapter as often as you like.
• Mock tests in the real format: 20 questions, 45 minutes, pass with 15
• Flag questions during a mock test and come back to them before you finish
• A readiness check: pass three mock tests in a row and you're test-ready
• Refresher suggestions for the topics you missed most

LEARN YOUR WAY
• English and French, switchable at any time
• Adjustable text size, from 17 to 25 point
• Higher-contrast mode with darker text and borders
• Calm motion that follows your device's reduced-motion setting
• Every answer shows an icon and a word, never colour alone

CALM BY DESIGN
No streak warnings, no countdown pressure, no confetti. The mock-test timer is there to help you pace yourself, not to rush you. When you get something wrong, you'll see why and the right answer, so you know it next time.

PRIVATE AND WORKS OFFLINE
No account needed. Your progress stays on your phone, and every lesson, quiz and mock test works without an internet connection.

FREE, WITH OPTIONAL AD REMOVAL
The app is free and supported by ads, which only appear between activities, never during a lesson or a test. A one-time purchase removes them for good. No subscription.

ABOUT THIS APP
This app is independent. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by IRCC (Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada) or the Government of Canada. Questions and explanations are original and based on the official guide, Discover Canada, which you can read free at canada.ca.

The test format can change. Always check your invitation from IRCC for the details of your own test.
